# LifeOS

Life admin on your phone: type anything ("IRP card expires 2027-01-20", "NCT due 10 feb", "rent every month", "kal dentist 4pm") and LifeOS sorts it, dates it and reminds you in time.

**Open:** https://novashiv11.github.io/lifeos/ in Chrome on Android, then menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**. On iPhone: Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

- Works offline.
- Your data stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
- Tap **Turn on reminders** to get a notification when something is due.

This repo holds the built app only. It is generated from the private source repo with `scripts/build_pwa.py`; don't edit files here by hand.
